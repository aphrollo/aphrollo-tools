`aphrollo report web` leads with where the A/B stands and a summary of the week's friction, escapes and cost, each against the week before; proposals are grouped by the change they call for; bars are page elements whose text stays readable on a phone; the session cost shows its split between lanes, coordination and unattributed turns, and the lane chart scales on lanes alone; long tables fold their tail instead of sending the reader to the JSON; evidence shows one replay command and folds the other seqs; numbers carry thousands separators. The report model gains `previous` (the window before, nil when it holds no events) and each friction row's `prev_total`. A coordinator's turn with no lane in reach is now `coordination` even when it never touched a lane; `unattributed` is left to subagent turns with no lane event.
